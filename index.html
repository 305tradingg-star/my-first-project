About David

David Small is a knowledgeable and dedicated Arizona Realtor committed to helping clients buy and sell properties with confidence. With deep roots in the Tempe, Phoenix, Scottsdale, and Greater Phoenix Area markets, David brings professional expertise and genuine care to every transaction.

Known for his clear and consistent communication, David specializes in understanding each client's unique needs—whether you're a first-time homebuyer, a seasoned seller, managing an inherited property, or navigating probate. His negotiation skills and personalized service have earned him a 5.0-star rating from 28 Google reviews.

David doesn't just facilitate transactions; he builds lasting relationships by providing professional guidance from start to finish, coordinating with contractors, service providers, and title companies to ensure a smooth, stress-free process.

Selling an Inherited or Probate Property?

Managing an inherited property or navigating probate can be overwhelming, especially if you're managing the sale from out of state. David specializes in helping families like yours through this sensitive process.

How David Can Help:

  • ✓ Understanding the Process: Clear explanation of each step from listing to closing
  • ✓ Coordinating with Family: Managing communication among heirs and beneficiaries
  • ✓ Working with Professionals: Liaison with title companies, attorneys, and other professionals
  • ✓ Property Preparation: Coordinating necessary repairs and home staging
  • ✓ Contractor Coordination: Managing repairs and maintenance work
  • ✓ Marketing & Negotiation: Professional marketing and skilled buyer negotiation
  • ✓ Remote Management: Full-service support even if you live out of state
